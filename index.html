Features

Sab kuch jo aap ko chahiye

Ek lightweight app jo aap ke system ko ek powerful media server mein tabdeel kar deta hai — bilkul simple aur fast.

Instant Local Server

Sirf ek click — aur aap ka system local network par streaming server ban jata hai.

Browser Playback

Client device par koi app nahi — sirf browser kholein aur stream shuru.

Multi-Device

Ek waqt mein kai devices connect karein — sab apni marzi ki video dekhein.

Wide Format Support

MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WEBM aur bohat kuch — direct stream karein.

Subtitle Support

.srt aur .vtt subtitles automatically load ho jati hain.

Video Dubbing

Kisi bhi video ko real-time mein Urdu, Hindi, English ya 20+ languages mein dub karein.

AI Dubbing

Video ko apni zubaan mein sunain

AG Stream Deck mein built-in dubbing engine — kisi bhi video ki original awaaz ko detect kar ke, usay aap ki pasandeeda zubaan mein translate aur dub kar deta hai. Real-time, right in your browser.
